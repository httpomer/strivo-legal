Delete Your Account

App Name: Strivo

Developer: WeaverSol LLC

You can delete your Strivo account and the data linked to it at any time.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Strivo and go to the You tab.
  2. Tap the Settings gear, then Account.
  3. Tap Delete account, type DELETE, enter your password and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Request deletion without the app

You do not need the app installed to make this request.

  1. Send an email to admin@weaversol.com with the subject Delete my Strivo account.
  2. Send it from the email address you use to sign in to Strivo (or the email on your Facebook account if you sign in with Facebook), so we can confirm the account is yours.
  3. We will reply to confirm, and delete the account within 30 days of your request.

Data that is deleted

  • Your profile: name, username, photo, bio, birthday, gender, height and weight
  • Your activities, with their routes, photos and videos
  • Your step history and goals
  • Your saved and built routes, and your privacy zones
  • Connections to other fitness services
  • Your follows, kudos and comments
  • Your club, event and challenge memberships
  • Your notifications

Data that may be kept

  • Messages and club posts you wrote are emptied, with their text and attachments removed, so other people's conversations stay readable.
  • Your contribution to a team goal's total stays as an anonymous number, so the group's progress does not change.
  • Clubs you own on your own are handed to another member, or deleted if they are empty.
  • Copies in backups and logs are removed after a limited time.

See our Privacy Policy for more details about the data we collect.
