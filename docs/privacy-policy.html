Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2, 2026

App Name: Strivo

Developer: WeaverSol LLC

Contact Email: admin@weaversol.com

This Privacy Policy explains what information Strivo collects, how it is used and shared, and the choices you have. It applies to the Strivo mobile app and the services behind it.

By using Strivo, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. About the App

Strivo is a social fitness app for walking, running and cycling. It counts your daily steps through Health Connect, builds a personal walking plan, records activities with GPS, and lets you follow friends, join clubs and challenges, compare steps on leaderboards, discover routes and send messages.

Strivo is free to use and shows advertisements through Google AdMob.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address
  • Password (handled by our authentication provider; we never see or store it in plain text)
  • Username and display name
  • Your date of birth, which we ask for at sign-up to confirm you meet the minimum age
  • The sign-in method you use (email or Facebook)

If you sign in with Facebook, we receive basic profile information that Facebook shares, such as your name and email address. We do not receive your Facebook password.

2.2 Profile Information You Choose to Add

  • Profile photo, bio and a location you type (such as a city)
  • Birthday and gender
  • Height, weight and stride length
  • Your main sports and preferred units

Height, weight, stride length and gender are used to estimate distance and calories and to suggest your walking plan. You can control who sees each profile field. Your birthday is hidden from other people unless you choose to show it.

2.3 Health and Fitness Data from Health Connect

With your permission, Strivo reads the following data from Health Connect on your phone:

  • Steps
  • Distance
  • Active calories burned and total calories burned
  • Exercise sessions (used for active minutes)
  • Floors climbed

If you also allow background access, Strivo reads your step count from time to time while the app is closed, so your profile and leaderboards stay up to date. This is optional and asked for separately.

Strivo only reads from Health Connect. It never writes to or changes your health records.

We use this data to show your daily totals, goals, walking plan, history and milestones. Your daily totals are saved to our servers so your history is kept and so your step count can appear to friends and club members on leaderboards, according to your privacy settings.

Strivo's use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Health Connect data for advertising, we do not sell it, we do not share it with advertising networks or data brokers, and we do not use it to make decisions about credit, insurance or employment.

You can remove Strivo's access at any time in Health Connect's settings.

2.4 Location

  • While you record an activity, Strivo uses your phone's precise location (GPS) to draw your route and measure distance and pace. Recording continues while the screen is off or you switch apps, and a notification is shown the whole time. Strivo does not collect your location in the background when you are not recording.
  • Your recorded route is saved with the activity and shown to others according to that activity's visibility.
  • Route suggestions use your last known position to find routes and segments near you.
  • Privacy zones hide the part of your routes near places you choose, such as home. We store the locations you set so the zones can be applied.

2.5 Activities and Content You Create

  • Activities you record or import from GPX, TCX or FIT files, including route, time, distance, pace, elevation and heart rate when the file contains it. An imported file is read on our server to create the activity and is not kept.
  • Activity titles, descriptions, photos and videos
  • Posts, comments, kudos and follows
  • Clubs, events, challenges and team goals you create or join
  • Routes you build or save, and goals you set
  • Reports and blocks you make

2.6 Messages

When you use chat, we store the text, photos and voice notes you send so they can be delivered to the people in the conversation. Messages are visible to the members of that conversation. They are protected in transit but are not end-to-end encrypted.

2.7 Camera, Microphone and Photos

  • The camera is used only when you choose to take a photo in the app.
  • The microphone is used only while you record a voice note.
  • Photos you attach are chosen through Android's photo picker, so Strivo only receives the photos you pick, not access to your whole gallery.
  • Share pictures are saved to your gallery only when you ask.

2.8 Connected Services

If you connect another fitness service to Strivo, we store the access token that service gives us and the activities it sends, until you disconnect it or delete your account.

2.9 Device and Technical Information

  • A push notification token, so we can send you notifications you have turned on
  • Standard server logs, such as IP address, time and the request made, used to keep the service secure and working

2.10 Advertising Information

Google AdMob and its partners may collect or process:

  • Advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • Device information
  • Approximate location based on IP address
  • Ad interactions
  • Diagnostic data

This is used to show, measure and improve ads and to prevent fraud. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Strivo asks for your consent through Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information above to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Count your steps and show your daily, weekly and yearly progress
  • Build and adjust your walking plan
  • Record, save and display your activities
  • Run the feed, clubs, challenges, leaderboards, routes and messaging
  • Send notifications you have turned on
  • Show advertisements
  • Keep the service secure, prevent abuse and enforce our Terms of Use
  • Fix bugs and improve the app
  • Respond to support requests

4. Activity Insights

If you turn on activity insights, Strivo's own servers calculate plain-language observations from your activities. Your data is not sent to any third-party AI service for this. You can turn insights off at any time.

5. Who Can See Your Information

Strivo is a social app, so some of your information is visible to other users, according to your settings:

  • Your profile and each activity can be visible to everyone, to your followers only, or to you only.
  • You can choose who sees individual profile fields.
  • You can choose whether you appear in search and whether people can request to follow you.
  • Your name and daily step count can appear on step leaderboards that other Strivo users can see.
  • Content you post in a club or conversation is visible to its members.

6. Service Providers

We use these services to run Strivo. They process data on our behalf or as described in their own privacy policies:

  • Supabase for sign-in, database and file storage
  • Render to host our servers
  • Mapbox for maps. Mapbox may collect usage and location data from its map software under its own privacy policy.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging for push notifications
  • Google AdMob for advertisements
  • Meta (Facebook) if you sign in with Facebook

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We share information only:

  • With other Strivo users, as described in section 5 and as you choose
  • With the service providers in section 6, to operate the app
  • With the people you choose when you share an activity
  • When required by law, or to protect the rights, safety and security of our users or the public
  • As part of a merger, sale or transfer of our business, under the same protections

8. Data Storage and Security

Data is sent between the app and our servers over encrypted connections. Your sign-in session is kept in storage protected by your phone's Android Keystore, and Strivo's app data is excluded from device backups.

No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please keep your login details safe.

Our service providers may store and process data in countries other than your own.

9. Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as your account exists, or until you delete it.

When you delete your account, your content is removed as described in section 10. Copies may remain in backups or logs for a limited time before they are removed.

10. Account and Data Deletion

You can delete your account in the app at any time: You → Settings → Account → Delete account.

This permanently removes your activities, routes, step history, goals, privacy zones, connected services, follows, kudos, comments and memberships. Messages and club posts you wrote are emptied, with their text and attachments removed, so other people's conversations stay readable. Your contribution to a team goal's total stays as an anonymous number. Clubs you own on your own are handed to another member, or deleted if they are empty.

If you cannot use the app, see how to request account deletion or email admin@weaversol.com.

11. Your Choices and Rights

  • Edit your profile and change who can see it and your activities
  • Turn Health Connect, location, camera, microphone and notification permissions on or off
  • Manage ad personalisation and your advertising ID in your Android settings
  • Ask us for a copy of your data
  • Delete your account

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal data, and to object to or restrict some processing. To use these rights, contact us at admin@weaversol.com.

12. Children's Privacy

Strivo is not intended for anyone under the age of 16, and the app checks your date of birth at sign-up.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

Your continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated Privacy Policy.
